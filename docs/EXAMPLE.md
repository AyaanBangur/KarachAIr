# Worked example: how KarachAir creates impact

*Snapshot from live data on 2026-11-15 (air reading 2026-11-16T01:00) by `npm run example`. It shows the pathway from data to community action. It does **not** claim a measured drop in pollution or heat.*

**1. The data says where help is needed.** Combining the satellite heat-risk ranking with live PM2.5 and live feels-like temperature, the neighbourhood with the highest need right now is **New Karachi Town** (need 50/100). Why: Heat risk #1 of 24 (index 75); PM2.5 30 µg/m³ (live); feels like 23°C (live); 0.3 m² green space per person. About **825,565 people** live there (WorldPop estimate).

**2. A student is offered a safe, explained mission.** "Cooling-point and shade survey in New Karachi" (2 hours, outdoor, adult required, locked if the air is hazardous). The reason is attached: Heat risk #1 of 24 (index 75) · PM2.5 30 µg/m³ (live) · feels like 23°C (live) · 0.3 m² green space per person.

**3. The organization verifies the work.** The student's hours are signed off and a certificate is issued, so the effort is on record.

**4. Coverage rises, and the report goes to people who can act.** Across the city, **5 neighbourhoods** are the priority today (those in high or urgent need, or the top 5 on a calm day). They are home to **3,894,262 residents (24.9% of the 15,648,606 modelled)**. With no verified work, 0 of 5 priority areas are covered. After this one verified mission, 1 of 5 are covered, reaching 825,565 residents. The report (live graphs plus this priority list) is what an NGO or school can use to decide where to send people next.

**What this shows and does not show.** It shows how data becomes a safe action, a verified record and a prioritised list for decision makers, and that each step is counted. Measuring the actual change in a neighbourhood is the next stage (a pilot with a school and an NGO); at hackathon stage we show how the impact happens and how it would be tracked.
