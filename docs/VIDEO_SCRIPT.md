# Demo video: 2 minutes or less (the Veritas structure)

Record the screen at https://karachair.netlify.app with your voice over it. No editing needed. Upload to YouTube as unlisted (or Google Drive with "anyone with the link can view") and open the link in a private window to check it works.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 to 0:15** The problem | Home page | "Karachi's air and heat hurt people who can't protect themselves. Students want to help but don't know where or what to do. KarachAir tells them." |
| **0:15 to 1:15** The demo | Sign in as a volunteer, open **Chat guide**. Type "Clifton", then "ask parents to turn off car engines" (reworded, not a button). Show the mission cards with the reason, accept one, click **Open in Google Maps**. Then switch to an organization account (or the Judge view) and show the **Report** with the line graphs and the "Missions volunteers are being offered" table. | "A student types in their own words. Hawa, our trained model, works out the activity and offers missions for the places that need help most, ranked by live air, heat and satellite data. The organization's report shows the same list and updates as the data changes." |
| **1:15 to 1:45** How it works | `docs/RESULTS.md` (the results table) | "We wrote 385 sentences, split them so no sentence is on both sides, and tested on 189 the model never saw. Guessing the most common answer scores 25%, our old keyword rules 59%, our trained model 86%. It is weakest on long messages, at 75%." |
| **1:45 to 2:00** Next steps | Closing card | "Next: a pilot with real students and real typing, and checking our heat ranking against thermometers. KarachAir: live data turned into safe action." |

Tip: say the three numbers (25%, 59%, 86%) clearly. Judges look for a baseline, a result on unseen data, and where it fails.
