# 3-minute demo video: script and shot list

Record the screen at https://karachair.netlify.app with your voice over it. Keep each part short. Rehearse once.

| Time | On screen | Say (in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card: "KarachAir" | "Karachi's air and heat hurt the people least able to protect themselves. Schools want to help but don't know where, or what to do. KarachAir answers both." |
| 0:20 | Home page, then sign in as an organization | "It has two sides: students and the organizations that guide them." |
| 0:30 | **Air quality** tab | "Live air quality for all 24 neighbourhoods, refreshed every ten minutes, with a guess at what is causing it and a ready-to-send alert." |
| 0:50 | **Report** tab, scroll to the line graphs and the "Missions volunteers are being offered" table | "This report is built from NASA satellite heat data and live air and weather. It ranks where help is needed most, and it lists the exact missions volunteers are being offered. When the data changes, the report and the missions change together." |
| 1:20 | Switch to a volunteer account, open **Chat guide** | "A student chats with Hawa: where are you, what do you want to do, indoors or outdoors. Hawa understands typos and different wording." |
| 1:40 | Accept a mission, click **Open in Google Maps** | "The student accepts a mission and sees where it is. Outdoor missions need an adult and safe air, and lock automatically when the air is hazardous." |
| 2:00 | Organization: **Org portal** verification, then the certificate | "The organization verifies the work, adds a description, and the student gets a certificate with their verified hours." |
| 2:20 | Open **Data & sources**, then `docs/RESULTS.md` | "We show our sources and our limits. Hawa understands 90% of wordings it was never trained on. Greenery explains only 2% of heat differences, so we don't over-promise. We have not run a pilot yet, and we say so." |
| 2:45 | Closing card | "KarachAir: live data, turned into safe action for Karachi's students." |

Tips: use the **Judge view** button on the home page if you want to show all organization tabs without signing in; zoom the browser to 110%; avoid showing real student names or photos.
